# How to Open a Pod Console?

1. Access your Cluster Interface on Gita:
    > ![image](/images/image160.png)
    >
    > ***Image 01: Panel - Cluster Selection***
    >
    > ![image](/images/image161.png)
    >
    > ***Image 02: Cluster Interface***

2. Click on the **Inventory** section on the left side of the Interface screen:
    > ![image](/images/image162.png)
    >
    > ***Image 03: Inventory Section***

3. Within the Inventory section, click on the **Workload** section:
    > ![image](/images/image163.png)
    >
    > ***Image 04: Workload Section***

4. In the Workload tab, select the **Pods** option to view the pods available in your cluster:
    > ![image](/images/image164.png)
    >
    > ***Image 05: Pods Section***

5. Find and select the **<u>pod name</u>** you want to access:
    > ![image](/images/image165.png)
    >
    > ***Image 06: View of Cluster Pods***

6. Click on the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the screen to view the available features for the pod:
    > ![image](/images/image166.png)
    >
    > ***Image 07: Feature Selection***

7. Find and select the **Execute Shell** option to open the console of the selected pod:
    > ![image](/images/image167.png)
    >
    > ***Image 08: Option - Execute Shell***
    >
    > ![image](/images/image182.png)
    >
    > ***Image 09: Screen - Pod Console View***

8. Alternatively, you can click directly on the three dots to the right of the <u>pod name</u> and then on **Execute Shell**, without needing to open the pod.
    > ![image](/images/image168.png)
    >
    > ***Image 10: Feature Selection***
    >
    > ![image](/images/image169.png)
    >
    > ***Image 11: Option - Execute Shell***
