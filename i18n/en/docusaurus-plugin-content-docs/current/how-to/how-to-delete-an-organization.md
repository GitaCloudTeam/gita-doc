# How to Delete an Organization?

1. From the [home page](https://app.gita.cloud/home), click on a cluster from the organization you want to change
   - Alternatively, use the search field in the top left corner, highlighted with the green rectangle in the image below
   > ![Figure 01](/images/image129.png)
   >
   > ***Image 01: Main panel - Cluster***
2. In the side menu, access **Settings > General**, in the bottom left corner
   > ![Figure 02](/images/image130.png)
   >
   > ***Image 02: Main panel - Settings***

   > ![Figure 03](/images/image131.png)
   >
   > ***Image 03: Settings - Highlight 'General'***
3. Click on **Advanced > Delete Organization**
   > ![Figure 04](/images/image132.png)
   >
   > ***Image 04: Settings - Highlight Advanced***
4. Click on **Delete Organization**
   > ![Figure 05](/images/image133.png)
   >
   > ***Image 05: Settings - Delete cluster***
5. In the pop-up menu, enter the organization name and click **Delete** to permanently remove it

> **NOTE**: Keep in mind that this will remove **all** recorded events associated with this organization. **This action cannot be undone**

   > ![Figure 06](/images/image134.png)
   >
   > ***Image 06: Dialog box - deletion confirmation***
