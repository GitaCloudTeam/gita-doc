# How to Change an Organization's Name?

1. From the [homepage](https://app.gita.cloud/home), click on a cluster from the organization you want to change
   - Alternatively, use the search field in the top left corner, highlighted with the green rectangle in the image below
   > ![Figure 01](/images/image124.png)
   >
   > ***Image 01: Panel - Main page***
2. In the sidebar menu, go to **Settings > General**, in the bottom left corner
   > ![Figure 02](/images/image125.png)
   >
   > ***Image 02: Panel - Highlight 'Settings'***

   > ![Figure 03](/images/image126.png)
   >
   > ***Image 03: Settings - Highlight 'General'***
3. Enter the desired name in the **Organization name** field
   > ![Figure 04](/images/image127.png)
   >
   > ***Image 04: Settings - Highlight 'Organization Name'***
4. Click on **Save** to confirm the change
   > ![Figure 05](/images/image128.png)
   >
   > ***Image 05: Settings - Highlight 'Save'***
