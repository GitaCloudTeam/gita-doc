# How to Exit a Cluster?

1. Access the GITA interface, choose the cluster you want to leave.
    > ![image](/images/image135.png)
    >
    > ***Image 01: Screen - Organization's main panel***
2. In the left sidebar menu that will appear, under Cluster, navigate to the Settings section and click on Definitions.
    > ![image](/images/image136.png)
    >
    > ***Image 02: Screen - Settings panel***
3. Scroll the page to the Advanced section.
    > ![image](/images/image138.png)
    >
    > ***Image 03: Settings - Highlight 'Advanced'***
4. Within the Advanced section, locate the Leave Cluster option, then click on Leave Cluster.
    > ![image](/images/image145.png)
    >
    > ***Image 04: Settings - Highlight 'Leave cluster'***
5. A confirmation message will be displayed on the screen. Confirm the action by clicking Remove.
    > ![image](/images/image146.png)
    >
    > ***Image 05: Dialog box - Leave cluster***
6. You will be removed from the cluster and lose access until someone adds you back.
